MEDICAL ALLOWANCE POLICY (SAMPLE - FICTIONAL)

1. Overview
The company provides medical benefits to help staff with healthcare costs.

2. Outpatient medical allowance
Permanent staff may claim up to RM1,000 per calendar year for outpatient treatment, including GP visits, medication and specialist consultations.
Contract staff may claim up to RM500 per calendar year for outpatient treatment.

3. Hospitalisation
Hospitalisation is covered by the group insurance policy for permanent staff. Staff should present their insurance card at a panel hospital to obtain a guarantee letter. Contract staff are not covered by the group hospitalisation insurance.

4. Dental and optical
Permanent staff may claim up to RM300 per calendar year for dental treatment and RM200 every 2 years for spectacles or contact lenses. These benefits are not available to contract staff.

5. Claim procedure
Submit outpatient claims in the HR portal within 60 days of treatment with the original receipt and the doctor's invoice. Cosmetic treatments and vitamins are not claimable.
