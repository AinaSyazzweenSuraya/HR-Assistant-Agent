TRAVEL AND EXPENSE CLAIM POLICY (SAMPLE - FICTIONAL)

1. Scope
This policy applies to all staff travelling on company business.

2. Accommodation
Hotel accommodation is reimbursed up to RM250 per night in Malaysia. Stays above this limit require prior written approval from the department head.

3. Meal allowance
A meal allowance of RM50 per day is paid for outstation travel of more than 8 hours. No receipts are needed for the meal allowance.

4. Mileage
When using a private car for company business, mileage is reimbursed at RM0.70 per kilometre. Toll and parking fees are reimbursed with receipts.

5. Air travel
Economy class must be used for all flights under 6 hours. Staff at grade G5 and above may book premium economy for flights longer than 6 hours.

6. How to submit a claim
Submit the claim in the HR portal under Claims, attach original receipts, and select your line manager as approver. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Late claims are rejected unless approved by HR. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary run.
